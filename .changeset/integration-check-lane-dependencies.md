The Lane batch integration recheck materializes its candidate beside the Lanes, so acceptance descriptors resolve the repository's installed dependencies (`node_modules`, `.bin`) as they did in the Lane instead of failing with `command not found` or a missing module and spending a redo. A redo that recreates a Lane first pins its unintegrated commit at `refs/imm/retained/<slug>/<task_id>/<commit>`, so settled work is never left unreachable.
